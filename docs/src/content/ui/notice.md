`tone` is `info` (the default), `success`, `warning` or `danger`; `error` is the deprecated name of `danger`. A danger is an alert, read out at once; the other tones are a status, read once the reader is idle. `label` is the message, and children follow it, such as a link.

## Compact

`compact` drops the box for a line of text, such as under a form's fields.
