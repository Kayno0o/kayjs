# What's new, older releases

What each release before 1.106 added, and the end of a newest release that was cut short (`(continued)`), newest first, each feature with where it is documented. The newer releases are in [What's new](whats-new.md); what a release breaks is in [Upgrading](upgrading.md).

## 1.106.0 (continued)

- A kay extension takes `ui`, a module whose default export is `defineUi({ ... })`, so a package restyles components for every app listing it, under the app's own `src/ui.ts` ([component libraries](component-libraries.md)); `src/ui.ts` itself follows no import or spread.
- `input(field=...)` takes its `type` (`email`, `url`, `number`, `date`, `checkbox`, `file` with `accept`) and `autocomplete` from the schema, and `select(field=...)` with no `option` lists the values of an `s.enum`, each unless the template writes it ([Forms](actions.md#forms)).

## 1.105.0

- kayjs-ui's `Badge` takes `color`, any CSS colour, in place of a tone ([Badge](/ui/badge#any-colour)); the new `IconTile` draws an icon, or a letter, on a rounded tile tinted in a tone or any `color`, and `Stat` and `Widget` draw their `icon` as one with `iconTile` ([IconTile](/ui/icon-tile)); an `AppSwitcher` app takes a `color` that tints its tile and icon, and an `@appIcon(app)` block replaces the icon ([AppSwitcher](/ui/app-switcher#a-colour-per-app)).
- The favicon, touch icon and manifest links, and the icons the manifest lists, end in `?v=` and a hash of the favicon source, so a CDN or browser holding the old icon fetches the new one after the source changes; the files keep their paths ([favicon](sitemap-search-fonts.md)).
- A redirect whose target has a query of its own (`'/calendar': '/?tab=calendar'`) keeps it and adds the request's params it does not name, the target's winning on a clash, in `redirects` and in the runtime set alike ([routing](routing-endpoints-and-base.md)).
- kayjs-ui's `Slider` and `AudioPlayer` fill their track in the accent up to the thumb (the danger colour when invalid, the muted when disabled): the range input carries `--range-fill`, its share from `0%` to `100%`, in its `style`, and Firefox draws `::-moz-range-progress`.
- kayjs-ui's `Slider` draws its value on the label's row, at its end, in place of a row under the track: the `value` part now comes before the `input` part in its markup.
- `deprecated-package-names` reports an old package name only where it is a specifier, a config key, a stylesheet path or a package.json entry, not a comment or a sentence in a string that mentions it ([deprecated lints](deprecated-lints.md)).
- kayjs-auth's `SignedIn` takes `require`, what `auth.guard({ require })` takes (a role, a list, a predicate), and an `@else` block for everyone who does not meet it, so `SignedOut` and `role=` are deprecated (`deprecated-auth-signed` renames `role`); `fresh: '10m'` on `auth.guard()` and `requireUser()` asks for the password or passkey again before a sensitive action, `auth.provedLately('10m')` and `reprove()` being its two halves ([The signed-in user in templates](../packages/auth/llms/signed-in-components.md), [Ask for the password or passkey again](../packages/auth/llms/sessions-and-guards.md#ask-for-the-password-or-passkey-again), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- kayjs-auth's groups share one set of verbs (`list`, `get`, `create`, `update`, `delete`, `revoke`) and every method sits in its group: `auth.organizations.listMembers()`, `auth.admin.require()`, `auth.captcha.required()`, `auth.proof.recent()`, `auth.tokens.hasScope()`, `auth.oauthServer.resource()`, `auth.email.signInWithCode()`; the old names work until 1.108 and `deprecated-auth-methods` rewrites them ([grouped methods](../packages/auth/llms/grouped-methods.md)).
- Every kayjs-auth method answers one `Result<Ok, Code, Fields>` in place of sixty `*Result` aliases, and a failure is a kay `Failure` too: an action returns `auth.password.signIn(input)` as it is, and its island call reads `{ ok: false, status, data: { code, message } }` (kayjs-auth's `passwords-and-mails` guide).
- An action's form readers are grouped under `form`, apart from its call state: `add.form.failed()`, `add.form.error('title')` and `await add.form.validate(form)`, where `add.pending()`, `add.status()` and `add.data()` stay on the action ([Reading the outcome](actions.md#reading-the-outcome)); the old names work until 1.108 and `deprecated-action-readers` rewrites them.
- A component's `slot` and `slot name` type the children and blocks a call may pass, so a component declares no `children` and no block it only renders with `slot name`, and a call passing a block with no `slot` for it is a `kay check` error; `children?: Snippet` beside a bare `slot` is rewritten by `deprecated-children-prop` ([Slots](templates-elements-slots-snippets.md#slots), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- kayjs-auth's `SignOutButton` and `SignInLink` replace the hand-written sign-out form and sign-in link, with no `features().prefix` in a template: `redirect`, `label`, children and a Button's `variant`, `tone`, `size` and `icon`, `SignInLink` taking `href` and `button`; `UserMenu` shows a visitor a link to sign in (`signIn={false}` leaves it out) and takes children and `redirect` ([signed-in components](../packages/auth/llms/signed-in-components.md)).
- A call from an island answers `{ ok: true, data }` or `{ ok: false, status, message, errors, data, error }` and never throws for a refusal, a failed handler, a network failure or a timeout, for an action and for `call()` of an API route alike, and `.orThrow()` is the throwing style: `await add(input).orThrow()` ([Calling actions from islands](actions-from-islands.md), [API from islands](api-from-islands.md), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- kayjs-ui's `Dialog` takes a `placement` (`center`, the default, or `right`): the side placement is the panel `SidePanel` draws, with the same parts, close guards and callbacks, and `SidePanel` and `FormDialog` are built on it, so `FormDialog` takes `placement` and `discard` too; a `src/ui.ts` restyle of a SidePanel's head, title, body or footer moves to the Dialog's ([upgrading](upgrading.md), [kayjs-ui](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md)).
- A top-level `const` computed from a prop or a signal follows it in the browser, with no `memo` or function written: `const last = items.length - 1` shows the new length once the parent changes `items`. `once-read-prop` now names only a `let`, a destructured `const`, one that awaits and a nested declaration; a `// kay-allow once-read-prop` keeps the first value as before ([Components](components.md), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- `endpoints` in kay.config.ts holds where the API and each feature are served, `{ api, openapi, mcp, health, metrics, jobs }`, every default under `/_kay` but the API's, and `openapi: { reference: { path } }` moves the reference page; the MCP endpoint is `/_kay/mcp` and the metrics `/_kay/metrics`, their old URLs answering until 1.108 ([Project](project.md), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- `kay lsp` serves a plain `.ts` endpoint and an `api()` module on the same shadow `kay check` types, so `params`, `query` and `guard` of a handler are typed in the editor with no generic written ([Guards](guards.md)).
- kayjs-ui controls that draw an input in a wrapper (Toggle, Checkbox, Slider, ColorInput, Combobox, DateInput) put `id`, `title` and `data-*` on the wrapper like every component, and the input takes `inputProps` (`deprecated-ui-input` rewrites the old form; [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- kayjs-ui components with a floating panel (Menu, AppSwitcher, BellInbox, AvatarGroup, DateInput, DateRangeInput, MultiSelect, Table, Combobox) take `panelProps`, and the ones drawing their own opening button (BellInbox, AvatarGroup, DateInput, DateRangeInput, MultiSelect, Table) `triggerProps`, typed as the element's attributes; `...rest` stays on the root ([slots and links](../packages/ui/llms/slots-and-links.md)).
- kayjs-ui names the same things the same way: every list a component draws is `items` (`tabs`, `avatars`, `entries`, `links`, `steps`, `icons` and `slides` before, rewritten by `deprecated-ui-items`), an `infiniteList` is `items` too on Carousel, Masonry, MediaGallery, VirtualList, LoadMore and Table (`list` before), TextInput, Textarea, Select, DateInput, DateTimeInput, TimeInput, MarkdownEditor and FileDrop take `label` as their name, Tabs takes `disabled` for the whole group, TooltipHost takes `onOpenChange`, and a tooltip has one documented entry, `data-tooltip` ([blocks, links and parts](../packages/ui/llms/slots-and-links.md)).
- Helpers of kay, kayjs-auth, kayjs-mail, kayjs-cms and kayjs-ai throw a `KayError` with a `code` where they threw a plain `Error` (`config-invalid`, `argument-invalid`, `outside-request`, `hook-timeout` and the new `helper-failed`, its `kind` naming a mail provider timing out or refusing), the helpers that run in an island too (`route()`, `every()`, `shortcut()`, `presence()`, `local()`, `navigate()`, `persisted`, `sanitizeHtml`, kayjs-ui's refused props) do the same, and each timeout says which one it is: `StoreTimeoutError` for a database call, `RequestTimeoutError` for a request, an action call's `timeout`, kayjs-ai's call, connect and idle timeouts. See [errors raised while running](errors-runtime.md#errors-raised-while-running).
- An `await` with no `catch` and no `try` around it shows an alert in place of its block once its value rejects, instead of nothing, so `uncaught-await` is gone ([Rendering](rendering.md), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- `src/await-failed.kay` replaces the alert of an `await` with no `catch` for the whole app, on the server and in islands, given the `error` and, in the browser, a `retry` ([Rendering](rendering.md)).
- A component's parts are given as their own attribute, `Card(parts={{ head: 'p-6' }})`, `class` keeping strings, arrays and objects of conditions, on a component with parts too; `Card.mt-4(parts={{ ... }})` is allowed, and `deprecated-parts-class` rewrites the old `class={{ head: ... }}` ([Styles and assets](styles-and-assets.md), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- `unkeyed-rows` covers every component, not only islands: a `for` over rows with no `id` needs a `key` ([Templates](templates.md), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- A link's navigation flags are named as the options of `navigate()`: `data-scroll="keep"`, `data-transition="false"` and `data-prefetch="false"`, typed on every element, and a misspelt one such as `data-prefech` is a `kay check` error (`unknown-link-flag`); `data-keep-scroll`, `data-no-transition` and `data-no-prefetch` are rewritten by `deprecated-link-flags` ([Navigation](navigation-from-code.md#navigating-from-code), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).

Releases before 1.105 are in the notes of their releases, at https://git.kaynooo.fr/kaynooo/kayjs/releases.
