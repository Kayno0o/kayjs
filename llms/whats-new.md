# What's new

What each release of kay and its libraries adds, newest first, each feature with where it is documented. kay and its libraries share one version, so one section covers them all; what a release breaks is in [Upgrading](upgrading.md).

## Unreleased

- kayjs-ai counts a signed-in kayjs-auth user, not their address, with no `visitor` option; `visitor` stays as an override ([kayjs-ai](../packages/ai/llms/limits.md)).
- kayjs-ai: a provider that stalls fails the reply with an `AiError` of kind `timeout` after 30 s without an answer or 60 s without a chunk, both set per call by `ask`'s `timeout` ([kayjs-ai](../packages/ai/llms/asking.md)).
- kayjs-auth stores: `members.remove` is `delete`, `grants.put` is `upsert`; `deprecated-store-verb` fixes it ([upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- kayjs-cms: `uploads.maxSize` over 128 MB less 64 KB stops `kay.config.ts` from loading, as Bun refuses a larger request body before the upload route sees it ([kayjs-cms](../packages/cms/llms.txt)).
- `sizeText(bytes, locale?)` from `kay/words` writes a size in the page's locale (`1.5 MB`, `1,5 Mo`); kayjs-cms's upload refusals use it, so its `cms.size.*` messages are gone and a size follows the locale on its own ([Locales](locales.md)).
- `deprecated-ui-prop` also reports a `Carousel`'s `autoplay={5000}` in a template, and `kay check --fix` writes `autoplay="5s"` ([kayjs-ui](../packages/ui/llms/charts-drag-and-media.md)).
- A duration written into a header or a cookie, `publicCache`, `cors` `maxAge`, the session and preview `maxAge`, is rounded up to whole seconds, so `'250ms'` writes `max-age=1` and not the `0.25` browsers ignore ([Public files](styles-and-assets.md#public-files)).
- kayjs-ui: a `ConfirmDelete` given a `variant` hands its `tone` to the icon trigger, so `variant="solid"` looks as the deprecated `variant="danger"` did, and `deprecated-ui-prop` fixes that one ([kayjs-ui](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md#dialog-confirmdelete), [upgrading](upgrading.md)).
- The browser's form check words a refused field through the same `kay.invalid.*` messages as the server, in the page's locale, Elysia's English for a key the catalogs lack; a page with a checked form sends them, the app declares nothing ([locales](locales.md#texts-from-kay-and-its-libraries)).
- The browser's form check words refusals through `kay.invalid.*` in the page's locale ([locales](locales.md#texts-from-kay-and-its-libraries)).
- Island props in single quotes, a third lighter than `&quot;` for every quote ([Islands](islands.md#props-to-an-island)).
- `health.timeout`, `images.remoteMaxSize` and `images.remoteTimeout`, `live: { connections, perMinute }` and `requestIdHeader` replace values that were fixed (5 s, 25 MB, 10 s, 20 and 120, `x-request-id`) and keep them as defaults ([Production](production.md#check-the-database-in-the-health-route), [Images](images.md)).
- Fixed values are options now: `health.timeout`, `images.remoteMaxSize`, `live`, `requestIdHeader`, redirect `status`, `false` for `proxy` and `preview` ([config](../docs/src/content/docs/reference/config.md)).
- Fixed values are options: `health.timeout`, `images.remoteMaxSize`, `live`, `requestIdHeader`, `images.webp`, redirect `status`, `proxy: false` ([config](../docs/src/content/docs/reference/config.md)).
- `migrate --outdir` and `fonts[].subsets` match `build --outdir` and `weights`; 404 bodies read `Not Found` everywhere.
- `hydration` margins, a `kay:navigate` event after a swap, `// kay-allow-file`, `kay upgrade --dry-run` ([Islands](islands.md#hydration)).
- kayjs-ui: `TooltipHost` delays, `CopyButton` `copiedFor`, `SaveForm` `shortcut`, `FileDrop` `onReject` also gets files beyond the first; kayjs-cms `pageSize` ([kayjs-ui](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md)).
- `kay add` keeps the runner label a project was scaffolded with when it rewrites the CI workflow, and `deprecated-test-mount` also finds `await import('kay/test')` and `export { mount } from 'kay/test'` ([Project](project.md)).

- kayjs-ui: `MultiSelect` options take `icon` and `disabled` like ChipGroup's and Segmented's, a disabled option staying as it is under All and None ([kayjs-ui](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md)).
- `translation: hello` in the frontmatter of localized entries pairs translations that set their own slugs, `markdown.imageSizes` (or a collection's `imageSizes`) sets the `sizes` of a Markdown body's images, an image added after its entry compiled is linked without touching the Markdown, and `headingLinks: false` no longer counts a raw `<h2 id>` as a heading ([Content](content.md)).
