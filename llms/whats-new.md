# What's new

What each release of kay and its libraries adds, newest first, each feature with where it is documented. kay and its libraries share one version, so one section covers them all; what a release breaks is in [Upgrading](upgrading.md).

## Unreleased

- kayjs-cms has hooks before a change: `cms({ beforeSave })` runs before every save, publish, unpublish, duplicate, restore, delete and rename and may change `data` and `body` or refuse (`false` or `refuse()`, shown in the entry form and answered 422 or 403 by the API), `cms({ beforeUpload })` scans, resizes, renames or refuses a file before it is stored and `cms({ onUpload })` is told once it is; `onSave` also gets `data` and `previous`, and the activity log keeps uploads ([Before a save](../packages/cms/llms/loaders.md#before-a-save), [uploads](../packages/cms/llms/uploads.md#hooks), [hooks](hooks.md#kayjs-cms)). `onSave` is now told through kay's `dispatch()`: a throw is reported with source `cms.onSave`.
- The MCP tool `not_found` lists the paths visitors found nothing at, as `kay not-found --format json` does (`{ missing, total }`, capped at 200, reading only) ([MCP commands](editor-and-agent-commands.md)).
- kayjs-ui `ui()` imports from the package root like `auth()` and `cms()`: `import { ui } from '@kaynooo/kayjs-ui'` loads in a `kay.config.ts` under plain Bun, `@kaynooo/kayjs-ui/extension` stays as an alias, and `kay add ui` and `kay new` write the root import ([setup](../packages/ui/llms/setup.md)).
- kayjs-mail has [hooks](hooks.md): `mailer({ beforeSend, onSend, onError, onSuppress })` around a send, the queue and a bounce, `onSubscribe`, `onConfirm`, `onUnsubscribe` and `onBroadcast` on a list, and `onSend` and a `to` function on `contactForm` ([details](../packages/mail/llms/writing-and-sending.md#hooks)).
- kayjs-auth's and kayjs-ai's hooks follow the [hook convention](hooks.md#kayjs-auth): `onEvent` (was `onAuthEvent`), `beforeDelete` (was `onDelete`; it can refuse with `false` or `refuse()`, answered as `refused`), `onSignUp` logged rather than failing the sign-up, and an `AuthHookContext` (kay's `HookContext` and the `actor`) in each; in kayjs-ai `beforeRequest` (was `onRequest`; `false` refuses), `onSuccess` (was `onFinish`), `onError({ error, context })`, a `context` in each, and a hook given to `ask()` runs after the one of `ai()` instead of replacing it ([AI hooks](../packages/ai/llms/hooks.md)); kayjs-ui's `onSaved` is `onSave` on Form, FormDialog and SaveForm (whose call that does the save is `save`), `onSent` is `onSend` and AudioPlayer's `onEnded` is `onEnd` ([forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md)); `deprecated-hook-names` flags the old keys and props ([upgrading](upgrading.md), [auth setup](../packages/auth/llms/setup.md)).
- `session({ onCreate, onDestroy })` and the `onWrite` and `onDelete` of `diskFiles`, `s3Files`, `memoryFiles` and an app's own `FileStore` are [hooks](hooks.md) told `{ id | key, ..., context }`, a throw reported and never failing the request, and they feed `kay_session_events_total` and `kay_file_events_total` in `/metrics` ([sessions](server.md#sessions), [files](files.md#stores)).
- `stream()` has one return type, a `Stream` (a `Connection` with `start()`, which `start: false` no longer needs a separate type for), and a streaming action's `ActionStream` is a `Connection` too: both have `ended()`, `onData(({ value }) => ...)` and `onOpen()`, and `ActionStream.retry()` calls the action again after a failure; `EventReader`, `StreamRun` and `.on(fn)` are flagged by `deprecated-stream-types` and `deprecated-stream-on` ([API from islands](api-from-islands.md#event-streams), [streaming actions](actions-from-islands.md#streaming-actions)). `socket()`, `stream()`, `resource()`, `track()` and `call.track` take their transition callbacks as options and as methods returning what removes them: `onOpen`, `onData`, `onError` and `onClose` on a socket, a stream and a streaming action's answer, `onData` and `onError` on a resource, `onSuccess` and `onError` on a tracked call, each told one object (`onOpen(({ reconnected }) => ...)`, no longer a boolean, flagged by `deprecated-stream-open`), a throwing hook logged and never failing the source. A `local()` collection joins them: `unsynced()` is its count of writes waiting (`pending()` and `status()` keep their values until 2.0, flagged by `deprecated-local-state`), `syncStatus()` its sync states, with `error()`, `retry()` and the `onSync`, `onIssue` and `onError` hooks ([local collections](local-collections.md#what-a-collection-is)).
- `--- island` and `--- static` in place of `---` pin a component's role: `--- island` makes it an island on every page (where a component passing a function or a block with parameters was promoted only when the build found its chunk reachable), and `--- static` turns anything that would make it an island into the compile error `static-pin`, standing at the `on*` or `kay` value that does it, and is never promoted. What a component passes an island is read from its types: a function, a block with parameters, a symbol or an object holding one is the error `island-prop` (fixed by `--- island`), data and `any` or `unknown` say nothing, and `promoted-island`, now deprecated until 1.95 with the same fix, no longer warns of an action or a call that returns data, so `// kay-allow-file promoted-island` is not needed. `IslandPropsError` names the fix. `// kay-island` and `// kay-static` still work until 1.95: the new `island-comment` lint has `kay upgrade` write them as fence lines, and `island-marker` only reports `--- island` on a page, layout or special page ([Islands](islands.md#islands)).
- kayjs-ui IconPicker takes its icons as one `OptionsSource`: `icons` is a set, a promise or a function of `{ q, limit, offset }`, `search` is deprecated (`deprecated-icon-picker-search`, fixed by `kay upgrade`), and `onLoad(icons, query)` joins `onError({ error, query })` ([IconPicker](../packages/ui/llms/pickers.md)).
- kayjs-ui Tabs and Steps take an `OptionsSource` as `tabs` and `steps` (a list, a promise or a function), busy under `loadingLabel` until it answers and saying `failureLabel` with a `retryLabel` button when it fails, with `onLoad` and `onError` ([Tabs](../packages/ui/llms/tables-navigation-and-layout.md), [Steps](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md)).
- kayjs-ui RadioGroup, Segmented and ChipGroup take an `OptionsSource` as `options` (a list, a promise or a function), busy under `loadingLabel` until it answers and saying `failureLabel` with a `retryLabel` button when it fails, with `onLoad` and `onError` ([RadioGroup, Segmented, ChipGroup](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md)).
- kayjs-ui `SideNav(groups, links, current, label)`: a vertical navigation with group headings, `@link`, `@heading`, `@header` and `@footer` blocks, a collapsible rail of icons (`collapsed`, `collapsedKey` in a `persisted` cookie, `onCollapsedChange`) and a SidePanel on narrow screens (`open`, `onOpenChange`, `panel={false}`), matching the current page as SiteNav does; `NavLink` is now a `NavItem`, so `badge` and `disabled` work in SiteNav and AppHeader too. See [tables-navigation-and-layout](../packages/ui/llms/tables-navigation-and-layout.md).
- kayjs-ui Select, Combobox and MultiSelect take one `OptionsSource` (a list, a promise or a function of `{ q }`); Select asks a function again for each `query` it is given, and its `onLoad` and `onError` carry the query as the others' do ([Select](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md)).
- `resource()` renders `ready` on the server: the page asks the route in process as the visitor (guards included) and the island hydrates with the value, and a refusal or failure leaves it `loading`. It also takes `initial`, `seed: false` (leave a route to the browser), `key` (resources on one route and arguments already share one request), `revalidate: 'focus' | 'reconnect' | Duration` and has `mutate(value)` ([reading a route](api-from-islands.md)).
- Times an app reads are `Date`, named `<verb>At`, and epoch milliseconds stay in stores: `mail.suppression()` answers `suppressedAt`, `notify()`, `notifications.list()` and `pushSubscriptions.list()` `createdAt`, `cmsDrafts().recovery()` `editedAt`, a cms list row `modifiedAt`, `scheduledAt` and `endsAt`, a review step `doneAt`, the `ban` of an auth user `bannedAt`, a listed file `modifiedAt`, a `local()` issue `createdAt`, a Chat's conversation `updatedAt`, and a followed job's `runAt`, all as a `Date` ([conventions](component-library-conventions.md#reference)).
- kayjs-ai names models once: `ai({ models: { fast: 'claude-haiku-4-5', smart: { model, provider } }, default })` makes `ask(x, { model: 'fast' })` use that model and provider, `default` replaces `AI_MODEL`, and the models named `imageAlt` and `translate` draft alt text and translate; the environment alone still works ([kayjs-ai asking](../packages/ai/llms/asking.md)).
- `kay mcp --write` lets the MCP tools apply what they preview: `generate`, `add`, `fix` (`kay check --fix`) and `upgrade` take `apply: true`, and the new tools `build`, `types` and `lint` (which takes it too) join them; `test` answers `{ code, failed: [{ file, name, message, at }] }` and `dev_errors` takes `waitMs` to wait for the next change ([MCP commands](editor-and-agent-commands.md)).
- `eventStream(source, { id })` sends an id with each event, `lastEventId(request)` reads the header back safely, `stream()` sends the last id it took as `Last-Event-ID` when it reconnects (a GET stream now also waits out a 502, 503 or 504 during a restart), and `stream()` gets `onOpen(reconnected)` as `socket()` has, whose argument now tells a reconnect: [resume after a drop](api-from-islands.md#resume-after-a-drop).

The rest of this release is in [What's new, older releases](whats-new-archive.md).

## 1.91.0

- kayjs-ui and kayjs-auth depend on `@kaynooo/utils` 3.0.0, so kayjs-ui type-checks in an app that is on utils 3; an app still on utils 2 keeps it, as each package installs its own copy.
- Every error kay and its libraries throw for an app to catch extends `KayError` (from `kay/library`) with `code`, `kind`, `status`, `cause` and `name`, its old class kept for `instanceof`, and `kay explain <code>` documents them in the new `runtime` and `library` groups: [Errors](errors.md). An uncaught `SaveConflictError` now answers 409 and an `UnknownCollectionError` 404, where both answered 500.
- A library's config-time problems (a `cms({ collections })` layout, `roles`, `rules`, a rate limit) throw through `configError`, and each library's `llms/` lists its errors in one `Errors` table.
- `pgData(...).close()` and `pgHandle(...).close()` settle within about 5 seconds whatever a statement in flight or a locked table holds, so an app's shutdown is no longer held by a close that waits on the database: [queues](queues.md).

- A constructor is a noun across the libraries (`authCore`, `authGate`, `memoryAuth`, `pgAuth`, `cmsDrafts`, `cmsEntryForm`), every Postgres store and table helper takes one `PgSource` (a Bun `SQL`, a pg-orm `Database` or a URL; `pgLoader` takes it as `source`), `createXTables` creates, `xTableNames` lists names: see [Component libraries: conventions](component-library-conventions.md#conventions); `deprecated-library-names` renames the old names.
- Each library runs with no argument and takes the same three forms: `mail()` uses a `src/mail.ts` exporting `mail` as `auth()` uses `src/auth.ts` and `src/mail.ts`, `mail({ from })` and `auth({ store })` take their options inline, `module` names a file of the app, and `server: false` is the one escape for an app mounting the plugin by hand (`auth({ plugin: false })` is renamed by `deprecated-escape-option`): [kayjs-mail](../packages/mail/llms/writing-and-sending.md), [kayjs-auth](../packages/auth/llms/setup.md).
- `mail()` with no argument uses the `mail` a `src/mail.ts` exports, as `auth()` already reads that file: an app with `mail()` and a `src/mail.ts` now sends through that file's mailer, where `mail()` built its own from the environment (options given to `mail({ ... })` still win, and a file exporting no mailer stops the boot). `server: false` is the one escape for an app mounting a plugin by hand, on `auth()` and `mail()`; `auth({ plugin: false })` is renamed by `deprecated-escape-option`: [kayjs-mail](../packages/mail/llms/writing-and-sending.md), [kayjs-auth](../packages/auth/llms/setup.md).
- `page.fill` in kay/test waits for the field's island to hydrate, so text typed into a page just loaded or swapped in is no longer written away by hydration: [browser tests](testing-browser.md).
- `add.validate(form, field?)` checks a form in the browser against the action's schema and writes the messages into `add.error(field)` as a refusal does, and kayjs-ui's Form, SaveForm and FormDialog take `validate="submit" | "blur"` to show them on their FormField live: [actions](actions.md#reading-the-outcome), [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md).
- `validate` (or `validate="blur"`) on a `form(action={add})` shows the schema's refusals as text in the FormFields of the form instead of the browser's bubble: [actions](actions.md#forms).
- The FormFields (and Checkboxes, Toggles) a static page gives kayjs-ui's Form, SaveForm or FormDialog as children show a refused call's message with JavaScript, with no island of yours holding the fields: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md).
- Environment variable names follow one rule (kay `KAY_`, a prefix per library, vendors' own names kept, [Environment](environment.md)): `KAYJS_AUTH_DATABASE_URL` is `AUTH_DATABASE_URL` and `MAIL_INBOUND_SECRET` is `MAIL_EVENTS_INBOUND`, the old names read until 1.94 with a boot warning, and `kay env` reports one it finds.
- The forms 1.87 deprecated are gone, with their ten `deprecated-*` lints: `fileStore`, kayjs-auth's flat account methods, `revalidate`, `keep` and `keepFailed` for lifetimes, `onFailed`, `onSelect` on a Table, TreeView or Timeline, `.answer`, `refusalMessage`, `StreamStatus`, `ResourceStatus`, `ResourceFailure` and `api({ errorShape })`: [upgrading](upgrading-archive.md).

Releases before 1.91 are in [What's new, older releases](whats-new-archive.md).
