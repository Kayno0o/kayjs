## Tones

`tone` colors the badge: `neutral` (the default), `accent`, `success`, `warning`, `danger` or `info`.

<Demo name="badge/tones" />

## Icons and counts

`icon` draws one of the library's icons before the label, and `label` also takes a number for a count.
