# What's new

What each release of kay and its libraries adds, newest first, each feature with where it is documented. kay and its libraries share one version, so one section covers them all; what a release breaks is in [Upgrading](upgrading.md).

## Unreleased

- kayjs-cms keeps its on-page drafts per signed-in person and forgets them at Sign out, closes the activity log to a role that may not `delete` (see Upgrading), and asks for a click before a link from another site opens edit mode ([Editor](../packages/cms/llms/editor.md)).
- kayjs-ui roots write an app's `style` after their own declarations, so the app wins.
- The Styles tab and LSP show a part's classes written as an imported constant.
- kayjs-ui reads the app's own theme names (`--color-accent`, `--color-background`, `--density`), and the analytics consent banner now follows the app's `--color-accent`, `--color-surface`, `--color-foreground` and `--color-input` ([Setup](../packages/ui/llms/setup.md)).
- kayjs-ui's error toasts stay until dismissed (a `duration` gives them a clock back), `HoverCard` describes its anchor and closes on Escape, `ContributionGraph` and a `ScatterChart` without `onSelect` take the arrows between cells and dots, `LoadMore` takes `button` and announces the rows a page added, `Bell` announces a new notification, `VirtualList` requires `label`, and a tooltip repeating its element's name is no longer its description ([Basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md), [Charts](../packages/ui/llms/charts-drag-and-media.md)).
- `kay check` lints a `table` with no `th`, a `fieldset` with no `legend`, a hover handler with no focus one, and an email or password `input` with no `autocomplete`, and `kay build` a prerendered page with no `main` or `h1` (`unstructured-page`); the analytics notice carries the `lang` of its words ([Checks](checks.md)).
- kayjs-ui's `Checkbox` and `Slider` take a `ref` on their input as `Toggle` and `ColorInput` do, and the four share one busy, rollback and form-reset helper, so a parent echoing the value from `onChange` or `onInput` is treated alike by all ([Forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md)).
- kayjs-ui's `Combobox` list is named by the FormField label as its input is, and `ZoomPane` takes `+ - = 0` and the arrows only when the pane itself has focus, so a field inside it keeps them ([Media](../packages/ui/llms/charts-drag-and-media.md)).
- kayjs-ui's `SplitLayout` on a phone makes the list and detail `inert` under the open sidebar overlay, focuses a pane when it opens and gives focus back when it closes ([Page layout](../packages/ui/llms/page-layout-palette-and-trees.md)).
- kayjs-ui's `MarkdownEditor` preview puts the HTML it is given through `sanitizeHtml`, so a script, handler or `javascript:` link in the text never runs; `previewTrusted` keeps the old insert-as-is for a renderer that sanitizes already ([Forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md)).
- kayjs-auth's OAuth server serves and advertises an RFC 7009 revocation endpoint (`/auth/oauth/revoke`), an `oauthServer` with no `scopes` warns at boot, and an organization invitation stops working once its sender can no longer grant it ([OAuth server](../packages/auth/llms/oauth-server-and-tokens.md), [Organizations](../packages/auth/llms/admin-and-organizations.md)).
- kayjs-auth erases a deleted user's kay notifications and, with kayjs-mail, their address from its mailing lists, and `exportData` lists both; kayjs-mail gains `mail.subscriptions(address)` ([Account](../packages/auth/llms/oauth-passkeys-two-factor.md), [Mailing lists](../packages/mail/llms/mailing-list.md)).
- kayjs-auth: an OAuth token asked for one `resource` passes only on requests to that resource or under its path, and gets a 401 elsewhere ([OAuth server](../packages/auth/llms/oauth-server-and-tokens.md)).
- kayjs-ui has a `SubscribeForm` for kayjs-mail's list action, as ContactForm is for the contact one: a required email tied to its message by `aria-describedby`, a notice saying what the address is used for with a `privacy` link, and the proof of consent the list keeps ([Forms and Steps](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md)); kayjs-mail's own `SubscribeForm` and confirmation mail say the same, and `mail.list(name, { title, privacy })` names the list and its policy ([A mailing list](../packages/mail/llms/mailing-list.md)).
- kayjs-mail's `contactForm` queues the mail instead of sending it in the request, so a slow or absent relay no longer holds the visitor up or loses the message; it takes a mailer's `enqueue` rather than `send`, and a test waits for the mail with `outbox.waitFor(address, { site })` ([The contact form](../packages/mail/llms/contact-form-and-testing.md)).
- kayjs-cms drops its trash and, past `history.maxAge` (a year by default), its old revisions and activity on an hourly sweep, erases one entry from the trash, the revisions and the activity log with an Erase for good button (`delete` role), and `forgetEditor(name)` rewrites an editor's name there ([Personal data](../packages/cms/llms/history.md#personal-data)).
- kayjs-cms's unused-upload cleanup keeps the uploads a revision or a trashed entry still mentions ([Clean up unused uploads](../packages/cms/llms/uploads.md#clean-up-unused-uploads)).
- kayjs-cms's `passwordGuard` signs its cookie with the app's secret, ends a signed-out cookie in `store.rates` for every process, names the cookie `__Host-kay_editor_session` over https, refuses short and shared passwords in production, and refuses all tries after 100 wrong passwords in 15 minutes ([Signing in and roles](../packages/cms/llms/options.md#signing-in-and-roles), [Upgrading](upgrading.md#kayjs-cms-password-guard-is-bound-to-the-app-secret)).
- A `.ws()` route of an `api()` module takes 1 MB a message and 100 a second from one socket, closing it with 1009 or 1008 past either; `detail.messages` sets the route's own ([API routes](api-routes.md)).
- A request with a body to any route (kayjs-auth's, an extension's, the jobs page, your own `app.post`) is refused with 413 past the app's `maxSize`, 1 MB by default, and `maxSizes` in kay.config.ts or an extension's `maxSize` raises it for a route ([Body limit](action-protection.md#body-limit)).
- kayjs-ai translations are held when the reply holds a URL, an email address or a number the source has not, a provider that reports no usage is estimated for every step of a tool loop, and a provider's error body is read only for its first 16 KB ([Translating](../packages/ai/llms/translating.md)).
- kayjs-cms remembers a newsletter send in the app's `store.cache`, so a restart or a second process does not mail a list twice, and the Markdown preview refuses a text over 1 MB ([Sending an entry to a mailing list](../packages/cms/llms/newsletter.md)).
- kayjs-ai holds a tool that changes something until the visitor allows that very call: `Chat` draws Allow and Deny, `tool: { readOnly: true }` marks a read that runs unasked, and `confirm: false` lifts it ([Actions as tools](../packages/ai/llms/tools.md#allowing-a-write)).
- kayjs-ai's production default budget caps all visitors together at 2,000,000 tokens a day, warns at boot about a `budget` with no `total`, and `ai()` counts in the app's `store.rates` when it names no `store` ([Limits](../packages/ai/llms/limits.md)).
- A stop with a browser connected ends its live, presence and `stream()` connections, runs the `onStop` hooks and exits 0, a queue handler ignoring its signal gives its job back after 3 seconds, and `store.rooms` is closed ([Production](production.md)).
- A stop with a browser connected ends its live, presence, `stream()` and websocket connections, runs the `onStop` hooks and exits 0, a queue handler ignoring its signal gives its job back after 3 seconds, and `store.rooms` is closed ([Production](production.md)).
- A stop with a browser connected ends its live, presence, `stream()` and websocket connections, runs the `onStop` hooks and exits 0, a queue handler or schedule ignoring its signal gives its job back after 3 seconds at most (5 for a schedule before), and `store.rooms` is closed ([Production](production.md)).
- `kay build --static` refuses a plain param holding `/`, `\` or a NUL, and any file whose path would land outside the output folder ([Static export](prerendering.md#static-export)).
- A plain route param listed by `prerender` holds no `/`, `\` or NUL, and `kay build --static` refuses any file whose path would land outside the output folder ([Static export](prerendering.md#static-export)).
- The dev tool's routes exist under `kay dev` only, and all but its scripts answer this machine alone under `kay dev --host`, unless `KAY_DEV_REMOTE=1` ([The dev server](dev-server.md)).
- Task list checkboxes in Markdown bodies sit in a `label` with their item's text, so they have an accessible name ([Markdown](content-markdown-to-caching.md)).
- The server scaffold's `compose.prod.yaml` requires `SITE_URL` and `.env.example` lists it, so a deploy sends HSTS and canonical URLs ([New and add](project-new-and-add.md)).
- The privacy pages list every cookie and storage key kay and its libraries set (`kay_user`, `kay_editor`, `kay_editor_session`, `ksteps_<name>`, `kay-reloaded`, the offline cache), a test fails for one missing, and `analytics({ provider: 'umami' })` on Umami Cloud asks for consent by default ([Privacy](privacy.md#browser-storage), [Upgrading](upgrading.md#analytics-provider-umami-on-umami-cloud-asks-for-consent)).
- `analytics({ provider: 'umami' })` on Umami Cloud (no `host`) asks for consent by default, `consent: false` counts at once ([Upgrading](upgrading.md#analytics-provider-umami-on-umami-cloud-asks-for-consent)).
- The privacy pages list every cookie and storage key kay and its libraries set, and a test fails for one missing ([Privacy](privacy.md#browser-storage)).
- A loaded collection sanitizes its entries' `body` and `html` by default, and `trusted: true` keeps their raw HTML ([Loaded collections](content-cms.md#loaded-collections)).
- `sanitizeHtml(html, { relativeUrls: true })` keeps URLs with no scheme, and `collectionInfo()` reports `trusted` for a CMS ([Sanitizing](templates-elements-slots-snippets.md)).
