# kay's kingfisher

kay's mascot, flat and geometric, in the colours of the real bird. Each SVG is drawn on a 64 grid.

- `kingfisher.svg`: the perched bird, kay's mark, for light grounds. On navy, as in [`src/server/favicon.svg`](../src/server/favicon.svg), its beak turns `#eef2f7`.
- `kingfisher-dive.svg`: the diving bird, kept for motion (a hero, a 404, a loader). Unused for now.

`bun scripts/favicon.ts` draws `src/server/favicon.png` from `src/server/favicon.svg` after a change to it.

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy | `#17202e` | tile, eye, beak on light grounds |
| Kingfisher blue | `#1f8fd1` | back and head; art only, as it reads 3.16:1 on paper |
| Deep blue | `#1670a8` | wing |
| Orange | `#ef7d2a` | belly and cheek |
| Paper | `#eef2f7` | neck spot, beak on navy |
