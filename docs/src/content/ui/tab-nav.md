---
description: A tab bar whose tabs are links to pages of their own.
---

<Demo name="tab-nav/basic" />

Each tab is a plain link, so a reload, a new tab or a shared URL opens it. `current` is the `href` of the page on screen, and its link is marked as current and centred in the bar when the bar scrolls. For tabs that switch content on the same page, use [Tabs](/ui/tabs).
