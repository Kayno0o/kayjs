# What's new

What each release of kay and its libraries adds, newest first, each feature with where it is documented. kay and its libraries share one version, so one section covers them all; what a release breaks is in [Upgrading](upgrading.md).

## Unreleased

- Every store has the same two optional methods, `StoreBase`'s `ping()` and `close()`: `health` pings each store and answers 503 when one rejects, a stop closes each once, and `pgData(db)` has both. Files are a store too, `store: { files }` (the `fileStore` key is `deprecated-file-store`, `kay upgrade` moves it) and `AppStore` carries them when the app writes it: [Stores](stores.md).
- `navigate(path)` puts the current locale's prefix on a path as a link's `href` does, so `navigate('/second')` on `/fr/...` goes to `/fr/second`: [navigating from code](navigation-from-code.md).
- kayjs-ui callbacks follow one rule: state a prop can hold reports through `on<Prop>Change` (`onSelectedChange` on Table, TreeView and Timeline, beside `selected`), a press of an item is `onSelect`; `kay check` flags the old Table, TreeView and Timeline `onSelect` with the safe-fixable `deprecated-ui-callbacks` lint: [deprecated lints](deprecated-lints.md).
- `navigation` from `kay` tells an island where an in-place navigation stands (`status()`, `pending()`, `to()` and `error()`, an `ApiError`), `onBeforeNavigate(({ url, from }) => ...)` may cancel one and `onNavigate(({ url }) => ...)` follows each shown page, and `kay:navigate-start` and `kay:navigate-error` join `kay:navigate`: [navigation state and hooks](navigation-from-code.md#navigation-state-and-hooks).
- A page whose pattern is a collection's `route` prerenders and lists its entries in the sitemap with no `page` export, a feed needs no `item` or `title` (the fields of the entry and the collection's `title` stand in), and the collection's `modified` field gives each URL its `lastmod`, the entry's head `article:modified_time` and the feed its update times; `url` as a function and a feed item's `path` next to a `route` are the `deprecated-collection-links` lint: [content](content.md#show-one-entry-per-page).
- A feed item carries `content` (`content: true` renders each entry's body), `categories`, `image` and `updated`, `format` takes `'json'` or a list serving several formats, and the head of every page showing an entry of a collection with a feed links it, `page = { feed: posts }` adding the link to another page and `feed: false` leaving it out: [feeds](content-markdown-to-caching.md#feeds).
- Every kayjs-ui field control takes `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`) and `invalid` (the danger line and `aria-invalid`), and the field look is one constant: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md).
- Every kayjs-ui component with a `tone` (Button, IconButton, BareIconButton, RecordButton, CopyButton, Badge, Stat, Notice, Toast, Timeline, Sparkline, StatusStrip, ConfirmDelete, Menu and its entries) takes the one exported `Tone` and styles all six: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- `toast.warning(...)` pushes a warning toast, `ToastOptions.tone` takes any `Tone`, and `kay check` flags the old `muted` tone with the safe-fixable `deprecated-ui-tone` lint: [deprecated lints](deprecated-lints.md).
- kayjs-auth groups its account, provider and audit methods (`auth.account.delete(proof)`, `auth.oauth.link(...)`, `auth.audit.list()`, `auth.admin.createUser(input)`), and the `deprecated-auth-account-methods` lint renames the flat names: [grouped methods](../packages/auth/llms/grouped-methods.md).
- kayjs-auth checks new passwords against Have I Been Pwned by default in production (`breachedPasswords: false` opts out), and warns at boot in production when password sign-up is open with no `email`: [hardening and testing](../packages/auth/llms/hardening-and-testing.md).
- Every command takes `--quiet`, `--format json` and `--dry-run` or refuses it for a listed reason (`tests/command-options.test.ts`); `kay render --format json` prints `{ status, headers, body, ms }` and `kay upgrade --format json` `{ steps, changed }`: [project commands](project.md#reference), [check and tools](check-and-tools.md).
- `kay mcp` serves `fmt`, `generate` and `add` (dry run), `test`, `env`, `i18n_missing` and `a11y` as tools, and the llms.txt and guides of each installed kayjs-* library as resources: [MCP](editor-and-agent-commands.md).
- One hook convention for kay and its libraries: `onX` observes after X, `beforeX` alone changes or refuses (returning fields, `false`, or throwing `refuse()` from `kay`), runs use `onStart`/`onSuccess`/`onError`; `kay/library` gets `HookContext`, `hookContext()`, `notify()` and `decide()`, and `onError` in `kay.config.ts` receives the `source` of a hook that threw: [hooks](hooks.md).
- `refuse(message, { status, errors, data })` from `kay` stops an action with a message, field errors and data, answered as `ctx.fail` is (the `Symbol.for('kay.refused')` contract gains `errors` and `data`); `kay/library` gets `HookContext`, `hookContext()`, `notify()` and `decide()` for libraries' hooks, and `onError` in `kay.config.ts` receives the `source` of a hook that threw: [hooks](hooks.md).
- `kay explain island [file]` says whether a component is an island, why and the rule, or lists every island of the project; the editor's island lens reveals the line that decides it on a click, `kay build --format json` adds `cause` to each island and the dev tool's Islands tab shows why each is one: [islands](islands.md).
- Every failure of a call carries `status`, `message`, `errors` and `data` (the `ErrorShape` type): `ApiError` gains `errors` and `data` (`answer` is the deprecated old name), the failed branch of `track()` and `call.track()`, `FormFailure` and `LocalIssue` gain `message`, and a call that got no answer says `The server could not be reached` with the browser's error as `cause`: [calling routes](api-from-islands.md).

## 1.86.0

- kayjs-auth's flat methods (`auth.listUsers()` for `auth.admin.listUsers()`) and the `deprecated-auth-methods` lint are removed: [grouped methods](../packages/auth/llms/grouped-methods.md).
- The `hreflang` alternates in the head of a routed localized entry point at each translation's own slug (`translationKey`), as `alternate()` does: [locales](locales.md).
- kayjs-auth's prebuilt pages write their content in a `main` landmark (`kay a11y` no longer reports `landmark-one-main`), and keep an app layout's own `main` the only one: [setup](../packages/auth/llms/setup.md).
- kayjs-auth's prebuilt pages write their content in a `main` landmark (`kay a11y` no longer reports `landmark-one-main`), and `main: false` leaves it to an app layout that already writes one: [setup](../packages/auth/llms/setup.md).
- kayjs-ui's CollapsibleSection and Accordion tell their state (`onOpenChange`, `value`/`onChange`), Avatar, SiteFavicon, CopyButton and AudioPlayer report their transitions, Pagination takes `@step` and no `url`, TreeView loads branches from a function with `onToggle`, Menu `items` and VirtualList, Masonry, MediaGallery and Carousel `list` take an async source, MultiSelect and Select `options` a function like Combobox's, IconPicker a plain `icons` list, and MultiSelect, RadioGroup, ChipGroup and Segmented an `@option(option)` block: [open state and sources](../packages/ui/llms/open-state-and-sources.md).
- kayjs-ui's Card, PageHeader, PageSection, Widget and EmptyState take a `@title` block (PageHeader and EmptyState a `@description` too), Dialog, SidePanel and FormDialog a `@heading`, ConfirmDelete `@details`, Tabs, TabNav, Breadcrumb, SiteNav, Carousel and MediaGallery a block per entry (`@tab`, `@item`, `@link`, `@slide`, `@caption`); Card, CardRow, Avatar, Badge, Timeline entries and Carousel slides take `href`; TabNav gains icon, badge and disabled, SiteNav links `children` and `open`, MediaGallery `open`, and Notice `icon`, `@actions` and `dismissible`: [blocks and links](../packages/ui/llms/slots-and-links.md).
- kayjs-ui's Steps drives a client wizard: `onSelect(key)` with or without a `url`, a step's `state` (`error`, `skipped`), `icon`, `linkable` and a `stepLabel` block: [forms and steps](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md).
- kayjs-ui's Rating becomes a picker over real radios with `onChange` or `name`, taking `icon`, `allowClear` and `disabled`: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- kayjs-ui's Segmented with `param` also covers each option with a link, so a middle click, a new tab or a crawler finds it: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md).
- kayjs-ui's DateInput and DateRangeInput take `isDisabled(iso)`, a `@day(iso, state)` block, `onMonthChange` and `unavailableLabel`: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md).
- kayjs-ui's RowsField takes a column `type: 'number'`, `cell: true` columns drawn by a `@cell(column, row, index, set)` block, and `onAdd` and `onRemove`: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md).
- kayjs-ui's MarkdownEditor takes a `@toolbarExtra(tools)` block, `onImageError(file, error)`, `previewing` and `onPreviewChange`: [markdown editor](../packages/ui/llms/markdown-editor.md).
- kayjs-ui's ZoomPane takes `plate` and MapView `center` and `zoom` from the app, `onMarkerClick`, `onMove` and a `@popup(marker, index)` block: [charts](../packages/ui/llms/charts.md).
- kayjs-ui's BarChart, Sparkline, ContributionGraph, ScatterChart and LineChart take `onSelect`, `onActive` and `active`, the first four also `href` for a link per mark, BarChart and ScatterChart a `@tooltip(mark)` block: [charts](../packages/ui/llms/charts.md).
- kayjs-ui's MultiSelect, DateInput (and DateRangeInput, with the box in `onOpenChange(open, box)`), Combobox and AppSwitcher take `open` and `onOpenChange`, HoverCard takes `onOpenChange` and tells it when Escape or a detached anchor hid it so the app clears its anchor, and BellInbox takes a `@trigger(attributes, unread)` block: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md), [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- kayjs-ui's Menu, AppSwitcher and ConfirmDelete spread the attributes they are given on their trigger, CommandPalette on its dialog and Toaster on its root; only TooltipHost, whose `id` its tooltips point at, still takes none: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- kayjs-ai's `ai({ onRequest, onStep, onToolCall, onFinish, onError })` and the same names per `ask` run around every ask: `onRequest` may change the messages and system prompt or refuse with an `AiError` before anything is sent or counted, the others notify and never fail the reply: [Hooks around an ask](../packages/ai/llms/hooks.md).
- kayjs-ai's Chat takes `onSend`, `onReply`, `onStop`, `onError`, `onToolCall`, `onRegenerate`, `onEdit` and `onSuggestion` and `suggestions` as a function or a promise, ChatLauncher takes `open` and `onOpenChange`, and a `kai-chat-draft` event fills the field from outside: [Chat events and control](../packages/ai/llms/chat-events.md).
- kayjs-ui's SaveForm and FormDialog take `onError(error)` for a refused or failed save, ContactForm and SubscribeForm take `onSent(data)`, `onError(failure)` and a `@sent` block (SubscribeForm also takes extra fields as children), Combobox takes `onInput`, `onLoad` and `onError`, IconPicker `onSearch` and `onError`, RecordButton `onStart`, `onStop('press' | 'limit')`, `onCancel`, `onError` and the `recording` / `onRecordingChange` state pair, and ImageCropper `crop`, `onCropEnd`, `onError` and `fallback`: [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms-and-steps.md), [Combobox](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md), [ImageCropper](../packages/ui/llms/charts-drag-and-media.md).
- kayjs-ai's ChatLauncher opens on a link to `#chat` (its `hash` prop) and on a window `kai-chat-open` event, and window `kai-chat-send` and `kai-chat-draft` open the panel then reach the chat; kayjs-ui's DateRangeInput takes `open` (`'from'`, `'to'` or `false`): [Chat events and control](../packages/ai/llms/chat-events.md), [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md).
- kayjs-ui's ConfirmDelete takes `open` and `trigger="none"`, so a Menu entry, a context menu or a shortcut can ask for the confirmation, and Menu and AppSwitcher take a `@trigger(attributes)` block in place of the icon button: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md), [forms](../packages/ui/llms/forms.md).
- kayjs-ui's `Bell` takes `items` (and `unread`) for notifications from the app's own source, drawing for anyone, and `BellInbox` in an island takes a function or promise source, `onRead(id | undefined)`, `onOpenChange` and an `@item(note)` row: [Bell](../packages/ui/llms/page-layout-palette-and-trees.md#bell).
- kayjs-ai's Chat takes `@turn(turn, index)`, `@actions(turn)`, `@call(call)`, `@source(source, n)`, `@empty({ send })` and `@footer` blocks that replace how a turn, the buttons under it, a tool call (a result card), a cited source, the empty chat and the area under the form are drawn, and ChatLauncher forwards them: [Chat blocks](../packages/ai/llms/chat-blocks.md).
- kayjs-ai's Chat takes `images`, the paths of the site a reply may show an image from (`images="/files"`), so a tool's picture shows in the reply instead of as a link; images of other hosts or paths stay dropped: [Chat](../packages/ai/llms/chat.md).
- kayjs-ui's Table takes `expandable` with an `@detail(row)` block: a toggle per row opens a full-width detail under it, or under its card when stacked, and `expanded` and `onToggle(key, open)` control it: [tables](../packages/ui/llms/tables-navigation-and-layout.md).
- kayjs-ui's `attempt`, ConfirmDelete and FormDialog toast a `ctx.fail` refusal that is a string or carries a string `error` or `message` without a `refused` reader, and an `ApiError` answer's `error` is read like its `message`: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- kayjs-ui's `CommandPalette` takes `search`, a function from the query to a promise of entries (an action works as is) called once typing pauses, whose rows are listed under `searchLabel` after the entries matched, a stale answer dropped and a rejection shown as `searchError`: [Page layout, command palette and trees](../packages/ui/llms/page-layout-palette-and-trees.md#commandpalette).
- kayjs-ui's `toast.error(message, { action: { label, run } })` adds a button to a toast, busy while `run` is pending and dismissing the toast once it settles, and `attempt` takes `refusedAction(data)` for a refusal's: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- `kay editor install [helix|zed]` sets up Helix and Zed for `.kay` files (the tree-sitter grammar and queries, the language server run from the project's kay when none is on the PATH, a generated Zed extension), and `kay new` offers it as a step or takes `--editor helix,zed`: [editor commands](editor-and-agent-commands.md#set-up-helix-or-zed).
- kayjs-cms has `gitlab({ repo, branch?, token, baseUrl?, timeout?, fetch? })`, a saver committing to GitLab (gitlab.com or self-managed) in one commit per save, and `CMS_HOST=gitlab` picks it for a production server: [Setup and saving files](../packages/cms/llms/setup.md).
- kayjs-ui's RelativeTime takes `focusable={false}` to leave out its tab stop inside a link or a focusable row: [basics](../packages/ui/llms/basics.md).
- kayjs-ui's `infiniteList` has `reload()`, which loads again every row loaded so far and swaps them in at once, keeping the DOM of rows whose key is unchanged: [kayjs-ui infinite lists](../packages/ui/llms/infinite-list.md).
- `kay/test`'s browser page has `drag(from, to, { steps, at, to, hold })`: a mouse drag from one element to another, or a touch drag with `hold`, so a test of a sortable list or a board needs no raw `cdp` mouse events: [browser tests](testing-browser.md).

The rest of this release is in [What's new, older releases](whats-new-archive.md).

## 1.85.0

- A string with a `date-time`, `date` or `time` format no longer takes the 10,000 character default, so the OpenAPI document and the tool schemas show a date as its format alone: [API routes](api-routes.md).
- A component call merging a shorthand class, a `class` attribute or a spread with a condition object (`Plain.mt-4(class={{ on: true }})`) passes `kay check` under `exactOptionalPropertyTypes` on a component typing `class?: string`: [styles and assets](styles-and-assets.md).
- kayjs-ui's `LineChart` fed by a signal or a store updates in place on each change instead of redrawing, through chartist 2.2's `chartist/live` (peer `^2.2.0`) loaded on the first change of the data; `pointKey` follows a rolling series' points by label or position, and `animate` moves them from the old data to the new, off under reduced motion: [charts](../packages/ui/llms/charts-drag-and-media.md).
- kayjs-ui's `LineChart` no longer sets a global `Chartist`, which importing `chartist/compat` did.

Releases before 1.85 are in [What's new, older releases](whats-new-archive.md).
